# 25% Kicker on Extra Jobs — What One More Job/Week Looks Like
## American Leak Detection Jupiter | March 2026

**Concept:** Techs do their 3 jobs and go home. They're comfortable. The issue isn't motivation — it's that there's no reason to say yes when dispatch has one more call at 3:30pm. Making that extra job pay **25%** instead of 21% makes it worth picking up the phone for.

*Using each tech's actual avg ticket from 2025 ServiceTitan data.*

| | Per Week | Per Quarter (13 wks) | Per Year (52 wks) |

**Tom's comp goes from $98,000 to ~$105,176.** He picks up an extra $138/week in his check — that's noticeable. ALD picks up $21,528 in revenue it wasn't getting.

The 4% kicker costs ALD **$1,147/year** — that's the premium over just paying him 21% on the same job. For $1,147, ALD gets $28,704 in new revenue.

## The Question for Ken

The 25% kicker is simple to explain: "Your normal jobs pay 21%. That last call of the day — the one you'd usually pass on — pays 25%."

**What Ken needs to decide:**
1. What's the "baseline" that triggers 25%? Options:
   - After 3 jobs/day (simple, rewards anyone who stays late)
   - After their individual weekly average from last quarter (personalized)
   - After a team-wide threshold (e.g., 3.5 jobs/day)
2. Does this replace the quarterly bonus or stack on top?
3. Start with just Tom as a pilot, or roll out company-wide?

**Simplest version:** Job 4+ each day pays 25% instead of 21%. It doesn't change anything about how they work their normal day. It just makes that one extra call worth taking instead of driving past it on the way home.
